Who I am

I grew up in Palawan, where I first learned that leadership isn't a title. It's what you do when people are counting on you. That lesson followed me from student council all the way into the finance office I work in today.

I joined JT International Philippines in 2023 as a Bright Start Scholar. I started as a Territory Execution Representative in Sucat, then moved into branch finance in Las Piñas. Working both sides of the business gave me a fuller picture of how things actually run, from the commercial floor to the finance desk.

Outside of work, I'm finishing a BSBA in Marketing Management at STI West Negros University, still holding my Dean's Lister standing under the JTI scholarship. I'm also a co-author of a study on sari-sari stores and digital payments, the author of Diary of a Future CEO, and a Philippine Red Cross volunteer since 2022.
